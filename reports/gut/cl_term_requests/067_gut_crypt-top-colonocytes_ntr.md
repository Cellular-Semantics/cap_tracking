**Definition** (free text, with reference(s), please. PubMed ID format is PMID:XXXXXX)
A colonocyte of the human colon located at the crypt top, representing the
most differentiated, mature stage of the colonocyte lineage. Distinguished
by high expression of the water channel AQP8 together with CEACAM5,
CEACAM7, FABP1, TFF1, SLC26A3 and GUCA2A, consistent with peak absorptive
and secretory capacity at the luminal surface. It sits at the opposite end
of the crypt-villus differentiation axis from early colonocyte
(CL:4047018), which marks the earliest post-mitotic stage of the same
lineage, and is distinct from BEST4+ colonocyte (CL:4047052), a separate
ion-channel-expressing subtype scattered through the colon epithelium.

**Additional notes or concerns**

CAP's own curator rationale (from the project 1030 OLS report,
`data/2026_01_09_ols_report.csv`): "Mature surface colonocytes at the crypt
top, AQP8 high with SLC26A3, CEACAM5, CEACAM7, GUCA2A and TFF1." CAP's own
ontology assessment: "No specific CL term for late/crypt top colonocytes -
we separate these from other colonocytes to provide more resolution on
epithelial subtypes." Confirmed via OLS4: `CL:1000347` (colonocyte)
currently has only two children, `CL:4047018` (early colonocyte) and
`CL:4047052` (BEST4+ colonocyte) — no mature/late/surface counterpart
exists, so this fills a genuine, confirmed gap rather than duplicating an
existing term. CAP's current mapping for this label is the general parent
`CL:1000347` (colonocyte); this NTR does not propose changing that
mapping, only adding a more specific child term.
